# KrashPad

A writing and sight-word practice pad for young kids (pre-K to 3rd grade). One HTML file, works offline, no accounts, nothing leaves the device.

Open: https://mcrimaldi12.github.io/krashpad/

- `zone.html`: **Krash Zone**, the sight-word arcade: Trace It (strict stroke-by-stroke tracing, plus a keyboard button to add a word for good), Flash Cards, Match Up, Memory Garage, Fire Rescue, Police Chase, Wreck It, Dig & Dump, Turbo Race, Word Train, a crew of four original heroes, plus a detonator reward and a garage of vehicles to collect. Word list and progress are in the grown-ups panel (press and hold the gear for 2 seconds).
- `index.html`: the writing pad. Free writing is here; its tracing buttons open Trace It in Krash Zone.
- `letters.html`: the old letter tracer.
