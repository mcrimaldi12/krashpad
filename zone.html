On an iPad you can add better free voices: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices, English, then download one marked Enhanced or Premium (Ava, Zoe and Evan are good). Then pick it here.

How he's doing

Right / missed for each word. Red means tricky (the games show these more often). Green means mastered.
Everything stays on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, there are no ads, and there are no accounts. The voice is the tablet's own reading voice.

TYPE A WORD TO TRACE

The word is saved to his word list for good. It also shows up in the other games.
